****Chromatic Aberration Study Using Zemax Optical Studio
****

**Overview**
This project explores the analysis and mitigation of chromatic aberration in optical systems using Zemax OpticStudio. Chromatic aberration, caused by the dispersion of light through lenses, can significantly degrade image quality in optical instruments. This study involves designing and simulating various lens configurations to understand the behavior of chromatic aberration and implementing optimization techniques to minimize its effects.

**Objectives**
To analyze chromatic aberration in different optical systems.
To simulate and compare the performance of various lens materials and configurations.
To optimize optical designs for minimizing chromatic aberration using Zemax OpticStudio.

**Features**
Simulation of chromatic aberration using advanced ray tracing techniques.
Analysis of image degradation caused by longitudinal and lateral chromatic aberration.
Optimization of lens parameters to achieve superior color correction.
Comparison of different optical materials and designs for chromatic aberration control.

**Methodology**

1.Designing Optical Systems:
Designed multiple lens systems using Zemax OpticStudio.
Configured different lens materials with varying dispersion properties.

2.Simulating Chromatic Aberration:
Conducted ray tracing simulations to visualize chromatic aberration effects.
Analyzed longitudinal and lateral chromatic aberration for different wavelengths.

3.Optimization and Analysis:
Applied optimization techniques to reduce chromatic aberration.
Evaluated performance metrics such as Modulation Transfer Function (MTF) and spot size.

4.Results
Demonstrated the impact of chromatic aberration on image sharpness and color fidelity.
Successfully optimized lens designs to minimize aberration effects.
Compared performance across different materials and configurations.

5.Tools and Technologies
Zemax OpticStudio for optical design, ray tracing, and optimization.
Python for data analysis and visualization of simulation results.
MATLAB for additional computational analysis and plotting.

6.Prerequisites
Zemax OpticStudio (Professional or Premium Edition recommended).
Python with libraries: numpy, matplotlib, and pandas.
MATLAB (optional, for advanced data processing).
